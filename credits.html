Data

  • 2026 local body schedule and list of bodies: State Election Commission, Andhra Pradesh, as reported by The Hans India, TV9 Telugu and mycorporator.in.
  • Candidates: party announcements as reported by NTV Telugu, Andhra Prabha, Andhra Jyothy and The Hans India.
  • Results are shown only as officially declared. Always check apsec.ap.gov.in.

From nomination to final count

  • Two ballots per urban voter: one for the ward member, one for the Mayor / Chairperson.
  • Paper ballots, not EVMs — expect counting to run late into the day.
  • Reservations capped at 50% after the enhanced BC quota was struck down.
  • Deposit ₹5,000 (₹2,500 for SC / ST / BC candidates).
  • Chair / vice-chair indirect posts: 5–6 Nov (urban), 11–12 Nov (MPTC/ZPTC).
